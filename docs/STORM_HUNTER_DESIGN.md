# Storm Hunter — Stage 2

## Phase 1: Asset brief

An athletic, youthful but massive evolution of Primal Beast. Preserve the rounded rectangular muzzle, embedded forward-facing yellow eyes, thick thighs and shoulders, inward palms and segmented cylinder tail. Add first shoulder/forearm basalt armor. Use spheres/ellipsoids, cylinders, rounded blocks, wedges and corner wedges; no external meshes.
